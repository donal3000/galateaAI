02 · NATURAL RESPONSES IN CONVERSATION

Lisa in spontaneous conversation.

Listen for word choice, voice and how her reactions develop as the conversation unfolds. These eleven English interviews explore joy, sadness, anger, surprise, fear, disgust, trust, curiosity, confusion, concern and shame.

Only the interviewer’s questions are prepared. Lisa’s replies and reactions are not pre-written: they arise spontaneously from her character and her current internal emotional state. These recordings show a prototype under development.
