Lisa · a companion who remembers

A companion for people who need time and attention .

Lisa is a digital human on a screen. She talks, listens, remembers earlier conversations and expresses emotions through her face and voice. She offers companionship when care staff are busy.

Remembers: Lisa remembers the next day what was discussed yesterday.
Responds expressively: Her expression and voice are generated during the conversation.
Runs locally: AI computation and rendering of the avatar recordings shown took place entirely on a local computer.
Speaks German, English and Chinese.

Lisa is an AI avatar under development. The avatar images are rendered using Unreal.

We are seeking two to three pilot partners in care and clinical settings in German-speaking countries.
