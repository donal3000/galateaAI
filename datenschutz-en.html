2. Data collected

This website does not itself collect personal data such as names or email addresses and offers no contact form, user account or tracking. When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes connection data that is technically necessary (such as IP address, date and time, page accessed and browser type) to deliver the website and keep it secure.

If you contact us by email, we process the data you provide (such as your name, email address and message) solely to handle your enquiry.

3. Third-party services

To give this website a consistent appearance, we use the following service only if you have given your consent in the banner:

  • Google Fonts (Google LLC or Google Ireland Limited): Loads the Playfair Display and Inter fonts from Google servers. Your IP address may be transmitted to and processed by Google.
    Processing locations: USA and other Google locations. Google’s privacy policy

Without your consent, no connection is made to Google; the website is displayed using system fonts instead.

4. Legal bases

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG) for loading Google Fonts. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for the technically necessary delivery and protection of the website.
  • Contract or pre-contractual measures (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) for handling email enquiries.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR), where statutory retention requirements apply to us.

On request, we will be happy to explain which legal basis applies in an individual case.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, marketing or advertising services. We store only your consent-banner choice in your browser’s local storage (localStorage, key “galatea-consent”), so you are not asked again on every visit. This entry is technically necessary and is not transmitted to us or third parties.

You can change your choice at any time: Open cookie settings. You can also clear local storage through your browser settings.

6. Retention periods

Your banner choice remains stored in your browser until you change it or clear local storage. Connection data held by the hosting provider and data held by Google are subject to their retention periods; please see the respective providers’ privacy notices for details.

We retain email enquiries for as long as necessary to handle them and meet statutory retention requirements. The data is then deleted.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights to the extent permitted by law:

  • Withdrawal of previously given consent with effect for the future.
  • Objection to processing based on a legitimate interest.
  • Access to the personal data processed about you and receipt of a copy.
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Erasure of your data.
  • Data portability in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Complaint to a data protection supervisory authority, such as the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

For enquiries about data processing by Google, please contact Google directly. To exercise your rights, email us at info@galatea-ai.de.
